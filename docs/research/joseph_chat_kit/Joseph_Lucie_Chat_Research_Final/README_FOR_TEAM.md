# Joseph / Lucie Chat Research — Team Package

This package contains **only nine conversation excerpts explicitly approved for sharing** by Joseph. It does **not** contain the raw ChatGPT export, unapproved conversations, unrelated attachments, hidden reasoning, or a private shortlist.

## What is here

- `approved_interactions/` — privacy-reviewed J001–J009 excerpts.
- `assistant_responses/` — the approved assistant reply text, separated for analysis.
- `analysis/` — evidence-based findings, coverage notes, and product hypotheses.
- `manifest.json` — provenance, coverage, metadata availability, and redaction details.

## Source and privacy status

The source was a **sanitized ChatGPT export subset**, not an original full account export. All 12 attached retained-conversation batches parsed successfully: **169 retained conversations / 501 messages** were accessible. The team-facing package includes only the nine excerpts Joseph approved. Some excerpts contain visible redaction markers; these are intentionally redacted copies, and no unredacted version of those passages is included here.

Automated privacy review can reduce obvious disclosure risk but cannot guarantee zero risk. Please treat the material as user-provided research evidence and keep its distribution appropriate.

## Interpretation rules

1. `CONFIRMED` means the evidence directly establishes the stated behavior/fact; it does not mean the assistant answer was correct.
2. `INFERRED` means the prompt behavior plausibly suggests a preference or workflow; confidence is stated.
3. `UNKNOWN` means the approved evidence is insufficient.
4. Joseph's prompt-writing behavior is kept separate from assistant-generated style.
5. No recorded speech was supplied, so accent, spoken delivery, and video performance are **not analyzed**.
6. No approved excerpt confirms use in an actual video or confirms a Lucie workflow. Video alignment is therefore **unsubstantiated**.
7. Historical mortgage, insurance, real-estate, and ChatGPT answers are **not current professional/product guidance**. They are included only as evidence of interaction patterns.

## Important product-design caution

Recommendations in this package are hypotheses to test with Joseph or similar users. They should not be used to make automatic, unsupervised persona changes.
