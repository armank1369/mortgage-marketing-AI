# Coverage and Limitations

## What was successfully read

- 12 of 12 attached sanitized conversation JSON batches parsed successfully.
- 169 retained conversations were accessible.
- 501 retained messages were accessible: 245 user and 256 assistant messages.
- No exact duplicate retained conversations were detected during the earlier coverage pass.
- Nine interactions were explicitly approved and packaged as excerpts: J001–J009.

## Source limitations

This was **not** an original OpenAI account export. The supplied manifest describes it as a sanitized export in which private conversations were removed, direct identifiers redacted, internal metadata stripped, and only the current visible branch retained.

The sanitization report states:

- 1,172 input conversations
- 169 retained conversations
- 1,001 removed private conversations

Those figures leave an arithmetic discrepancy of **two conversations**. No attempt was made to invent or infer what those two conversations were. Accordingly, coverage is complete for the **169 attached retained conversations**, not for the original account history.

## Metadata unavailable

The retained conversation objects contained titles and message role/content but no original dates, conversation IDs, message IDs, or alternate-branch metadata. Those fields are left null in J001–J009 rather than reconstructed.

## Attachments and video

The sanitized asset-name inventory records generic attachment mappings and includes one generic video entry, but the actual video was not part of the supplied research material and no approved interaction links to it. No video-performance claim is made.

## Privacy and publication caution

The final ZIP contains only the approved excerpts. Pre-existing redaction markers were preserved, and additional approved redactions were applied where needed. Automated privacy review is not perfect; the user should skim the package before external sharing.

## Factual-status caution

Assistant answers contained in the excerpts are historical conversation evidence. They have **not** been re-verified for current mortgage programs, underwriting practice, laws, regulation, insurance servicing policy, commercial brokerage practice, or ChatGPT product behavior.
